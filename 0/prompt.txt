## Product: Aftermath

**Autonomous recovery for broken business operations.**

Aftermath identifies cases where customers successfully complete payments but fail to receive their purchased SaaS entitlements.

An AI agent investigates the discrepancy, verifies payment evidence, selects appropriate recovery actions, safely executes them, and independently confirms resolution.

This must be a functional autonomous agent, NOT a scripted workflow masquerading as one.

## Core requirements

The autonomous agent must:

1. Detect payment-entitlement inconsistencies.
2. Investigate using actual tools and external evidence.
3. Independently verify Paystack payment status.
4. Validate expected amount, currency, customer and order identity.
5. Inspect current entitlement state.
6. Decide between recovery, retry, verification, or escalation.
7. Execute authorized provisioning actions with idempotency guarantees.
8. Persist progress across interruptions.
9. Reconcile uncertain side effects before retrying.
10. Independently verify the final outcome.
11. Produce an auditable incident history with neatlogs traces.

Agent reasoning and deterministic authorization must be separate. The LLM must never be the final authority for payment validity or dangerous mutations.

## Four mandatory acceptance scenarios

A. Successful payment with missing entitlement → autonomous investigation, recovery and verification.

B. Temporary provisioning failure → persistent retry, eventual recovery and verification.

C. Invalid, mismatched or uncertain payment → safe refusal and escalation.

D. Duplicate webhooks or repeated recovery attempts → no duplicate provisioning or incorrect state transitions.

Tests must cover real state transitions and failure boundaries, not merely mocked success responses.
